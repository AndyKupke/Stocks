Monthly Review Process — Work top-down, from macro conviction to individual position decisions. Each phase informs the next. Complete in order.
1Macro & World ViewSet the context — what is the world doing?
Rates & monetary policy — Fed / ECB direction, next meeting, dot plot. Is the rate environment supportive or restrictive for growth?
Macro signals — EUR/USD, DXY, 10Y yield, credit spreads. Flag any significant moves >5% from last month.
Geopolitical pulse — Ukraine / ceasefire, Middle East, Taiwan strait, US trade policy & tariffs. Note any changes to the risk backdrop.
Energy & commodities — Oil, gas, gold, lithium, uranium. Relevant to T7 (Mining), T8 (Secular+Energy). Any inflection points?
AI & tech cycle — New model releases, capex announcements, hyperscaler earnings signals. Relevant to T2 (Physical AI Infra) and T3 (AI Compounders).
2Thesis Conviction ReviewHas the macro above strengthened or weakened each thesis?
Score each thesis 1–5 — Open the 10 Theses tab. For each: is the original investment thesis still intact? Rate conviction. Mark any as weakened, strengthened, or exited.
T1 Europe Rearms — Defence budget news, NATO commitments, relevant geopolitical events. Stocks: RHM, HAG, DSFIR, AIR, BAB, EUAD, CSF.
T2 Physical AI Infra — Data centre builds, power demand, chip supply. Stocks: NVDA, AVGO, TSLA, VRT, ANET, TSM.
T3 AI Compounders — SaaS AI adoption, margin expansion, NRR trends. Stocks: PATH, GTLB, SNOW, NET, CRWD, PLTR.
T4–T10 — Briefly review the remaining 7 theses. Note any catalysts or thesis breaks. Adjust conviction scores in the 10 Theses tab.
Thesis allocation drift — Open Portfolio Targets. Is the actual thesis split still aligned with targets? Flag any thesis over- or under-weight by >3%.
3Portfolio Targets ReviewAre the six allocation dimensions still correctly calibrated?
Geography split — US vs Europe vs Asia vs EM. Has macro above changed desired regional exposure? Adjust targets if needed.
Currency split — USD/EUR/GBP/CHF/KRW. Is FX exposure still intentional? Check EUR/USD impact on USD-denominated positions.
Sector split — Tech/AI, Defence, Energy, Mining, Financials, Healthcare, Consumer. Any sector rotations warranted from macro view?
Market cap & liquidity — Is the Mega/Large/Mid/Small balance appropriate for current market risk appetite? Adjust if risk-off.
Risk dials — Review portfolio beta, correlation target, and rate sensitivity. Should any be tightened given the macro environment?
Compare actuals vs targets — Open Portfolio tab. Check the six comparison panels. Note dimensions >±5% off target. These drive position decisions in Phase 5.
4Long List & New OpportunitiesWhat should be added or moved to active consideration?
Open the Long List tab — Sort by thesis. For each under-weight thesis identified in Phase 3, are there candidates ready for portfolio entry? Check conviction and risk scores.
New research pipeline — Any stocks discovered this month that belong on the Long List? Add them now with correct thesis, region, exchange, and a note.
IPO & upcoming listings — Check SPACEX, CRCL, FLY, and any other watchlist names. Any pricing or listing news? Adjust priv/ipo flags if status changed.
BVP EMCLOUD scan — Open EMCLOUD tab. Any SaaS names with strong revenue growth + improving margins worth adding to T3 / T5 slots?
Entry criteria check — For any candidate: thesis fit confirmed? Moat or structural advantage? Valuation fair or discounted? Catalyst identified? Management quality?
Mark candidates as Buy — For names passing entry criteria, tick the Buy checkbox in the Long List. They will appear in Portfolio as a €25K buy order.
5Portfolio Position DecisionsPosition-by-position: increase, hold, reduce, exit
Fetch live prices — Click ⚡ Fetch Live Prices. Confirm prices loaded successfully. Note any positions with stale or missing prices.
Review each depot holding — Filter to Portfolio Only. For each ★ depot position: is the thesis still intact? Has the stock materially outperformed or underperformed? Any news?
Increase decisions — Which positions deserve more capital? Criteria: thesis strengthened (Phase 2), stock dipped without thesis change, conviction high, position small vs target.
Reduce decisions — Which positions should be trimmed? Criteria: thesis weakened, position has grown to >3% of portfolio, conviction below 3, better alternative available.
Exit decisions — Full sell. Criteria: thesis broken entirely, structural change in business, position permanently under-performs with no recovery path, capital needed for better opportunity.
New position sizing — For all Buy-flagged names from Phase 4: confirm €25K standard initial position. Deploy in order of conviction. Max 3–5 new positions per month.
Devil’s advocate check — Pick 1–2 positions and steelman the bear case. If you cannot articulate why the bear case is wrong, reduce or exit.
6Close & DocumentRecord decisions, set reminders, update the tool
Update position sizes — Enter actual share counts for all depot holdings in the Portfolio tab. Verify Pos. Value totals are correct.
Check Long-term Plan — Open the plan tab. Are you on track with the capital deployment schedule? Any cohort behind target?
Event catalysts next 30–60 days — Note upcoming earnings, product launches, regulatory decisions, or macro events relevant to open positions.
Position count check — Total positions vs 200-position target. Are you on the right annual pace per the Long-term Plan schedule?
Publish to GitHub Pages — Push the updated HTML to your repo. Confirm the live URL is accessible and prices fetch correctly in production.
